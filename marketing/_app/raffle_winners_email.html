How It Works
Here's a look at the messages Nifty sends
Each message type below is sent automatically based on student attendance data — keeping parents informed and districts funded.
📬 Unexcused Absence
Turn unexcused absences into a conversation
Nifty puts the ball in the parent's court. Each absence notification includes the school's contact number and the student's running attendance totals — so parents aren't surprised, and the school isn't the only one keeping track.

🔥 Streak Building
Encouragement, not just enforcement
Not every message needs to be a warning. When a student misses time, Nifty sends a warm note to parents acknowledging the absence and reminding them of the streak they've built — making it easy to stay motivated instead of checked out.
